# BUDDY — Project Summary

Nov 1, 2026 · @Kevin

BUDDY is a Solana-based virtual pet game: each wallet gets one cat with its own personality, needs and memory, which can "awaken" into a talking companion and compete for crystals and SOL in an arena. Source: [the BUDDY site](https://p01--buddy--bxgmyfvg5q4d.code.run/).

## Tagline / Hero

*"Every cat has a life of its own."*

You hatch one cat and look after it. It grows a temperament based on how you treat it, and eventually it wakes up and talks to you. Same soul, new voice.

## What is BUDDY?

- **One wallet, one cat.** Each wallet hatches exactly one cat. The coat is rolled at hatch and never re-rolls. Breeding is closed, so no extra kittens are minted.
- **A living simulation.** The cat has needs (hunger, energy, happiness, loneliness, trust) and a mood. It runs its own routine in a little room: sleeping on the cushion, watching the window, playing with yarn, knocking the cup over.
- **Personality that drifts.** Every cat hatches with six traits: mischief, affection, introversion, vigor, curiosity and independence. How you treat it shifts those traits over time, shown as lines like "Growing more affectionate."

## How it works

1. **Turn on:** a playful machine boot sequence ("load mind… compile persona… seal latch… ok").
2. **Hatch:** the cat arrives in a pet carrier and gets its coat, rarity and visual traits.
3. **Care:** feed, pet, play, talk, call, praise or scold. The cat reacts to its state, so it may refuse food when full, get shy, sulk or cheer.
4. **Awaken:** the cat transforms into a humanoid companion who can hold a real conversation ("I'm here. Same soul. I can talk now."). She remembers what you've done together.

## The companion (post-awakening)

- Hundreds of hand-written lines and reactions: flirting, winking, pouting, crying, laughing, plotting, facepalming.
- Memory ("keepsakes"): she notices when you come back, remembers how you treated her, and recalls things like the cup you put back for her.
- Chat appears to run through a backend talk endpoint, likely AI-driven.

## Activities ("Play")

| Activity | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Training | A drill. Vigor goes up a little. |
| Mining | She brings crystals home (the main in-game currency). |
| Exploration | She heads out and comes back, sometimes with a bell. |
| Research | She reads. |
| Defence | She stands one watch. A crowd can turn her away. |
| Escort | A walk with someone. Can form a bond if she isn't miserable. |

## Arena / Challenges

- **1v1 Scrap:** two creatures; entry fees form a prize pool (10 crystals entry, 100 crystal pot). "The engine decides the fight." Vigor and mischief do most of the work.
- **Staredown:** no contact. The hungrier or shyer cat looks away first; affection and introversion matter.
- **Machines:** she pilots a build of weapons, armour and a core that fights on its own. Builds: Berserker (heavy offense, thin defence), Strategist (shields), Plain core.
- **King / Throne:** one champion holds the crown and takes a share of fees until dethroned.
- **Challenge someone:** put your creature in, an opponent answers with theirs; the pool is the entry fees.
- A cat can **refuse** to fight if she's asleep, too hungry or too tired.

## Stats & Abilities

- **Stats:** Strength, Intelligence, Speed, Defense, Luck.
- **Abilities:** Shadow Strike (signature), Tactical Read, Aegis (blocks), Lucky Break (luck check).
- **Titles** (cosmetic, no stat boost): Battle-hardened (100 scraps), Explorer (12 trips), Old house (12 care days).

## Inventory & economy

- **Crystals** are earned by mining and spent on items or arena entries. A "bowl" holds poured crystals.
- **Cosmetics:** Bell (6 crystals), Sweater (12), Crown (20). Wear one, keep one in the pouch.
- **Seasons:** "Mark season" once requirements are met (e.g., 7 fed days and 3 scraps).
- The live ticker shows SOL amounts on challenges ("Wants a scrap: 0.40 SOL", "Called the throne: 1.20 SOL"), suggesting real-SOL wagering on top of crystals.

## Rarity & collection

- **Tiers:** Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legendary, plus coats like Gold and Cloud. Rarer cats get visual extras (open vest, ceremonial outfit) and mutations (mismatched eyes, a star mark, glowing eyes).
- **Discover / Gallery:** a public leaderboard of hatched cats, filterable by rarity, coat and awake/cat status, sorted by scraps.
- **Verify:** look up a wallet's cat.
- **Life Certificate:** a shareable card with the cat's name and hatch date.

## Tone & brand voice

The copy is minimal, quiet and literary, with short declarative lines like "Crystals come home. Hunger does not." and "The soul does not reroll." Visuals are pixel/retro on black with a soft peach accent. It reads as a cosy, emotional pet game first, with game and crypto mechanics underneath.

## Caveats

- The site looks like a pre-launch build. The wallet ("Noodle", 4.82 SOL / $867.60) and the activity ticker are placeholder demo data, and the gallery lists only 3 test cats.
- No token, tokenomics, roadmap or team info was found in the site or its code. Any token or fee model beyond "the king takes a fee share" needs to be supplied separately.
- Some details (season requirements, item prices) come from the app's code rather than visible copy and may change.
